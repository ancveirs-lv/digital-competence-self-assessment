# Digital Competence Self-Assessment

> This is an independent adaptation inspired by DigComp 3.0 concepts. It is not an official European Commission assessment and does not imply Commission endorsement.

## Response states

- `UNKNOWN` — Not sure: I am not sure whether I can perform this capability.
- `NEEDS_HELP` — I need help: I can perform it only with material assistance.
- `INDEPENDENT` — I can do this independently: I can perform it independently in ordinary situations.
- `CONFIDENT` — I can do this confidently and explain it: I can perform it independently, explain the approach and adapt it to a new situation.

## Assessment

### Information and data literacy

#### D01

I can formulate an information need before choosing a search or AI tool.

**Recommended action:** State the question, required evidence and acceptable source types before searching.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D02

I compare source quality, date, authorship and evidence before relying on online information.

**Recommended action:** Use a repeatable source-check routine on consequential claims.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D03

I can organise files and data so that I can find the current version later.

**Recommended action:** Adopt simple naming, versioning and folder rules.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D04

I can interpret basic tables and charts without confusing correlation with proof.

**Recommended action:** Identify population, period, units and what the data does not establish.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D05

When AI gives factual information, I know when and how to verify it.

**Recommended action:** Verify consequential AI claims against primary or authoritative sources.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

### Communication and collaboration

#### D06

I choose communication channels according to sensitivity, urgency and audience.

**Recommended action:** Check recipient, channel and sharing permissions before sending sensitive material.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D07

I can collaborate on shared documents without losing track of versions or ownership.

**Recommended action:** Use version history and clear edit/review responsibilities.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D08

I manage what my digital profiles reveal and who can access shared information.

**Recommended action:** Review profile visibility, old sharing links and permissions.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D09

I recognise when identity, tone or context may be ambiguous in digital communication.

**Recommended action:** Verify unusual or consequential requests through another channel.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D10

I review AI-assisted messages before sending them as if they were my own.

**Recommended action:** Check facts, tone, confidential information and unintended claims.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

### Digital content creation

#### D11

I can create and edit digital content in formats appropriate to purpose and audience.

**Recommended action:** Practise presenting the same information in two suitable formats.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D12

I consider copyright, licensing and attribution before reusing digital material.

**Recommended action:** Check reuse rights and record the source before publishing.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D13

I can make common documents reasonably accessible to different users.

**Recommended action:** Use meaningful headings, readable structure and alternative text where needed.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D14

I can use simple automation or reusable templates without losing control of the result.

**Recommended action:** Automate one repeatable task and define what still needs human checking.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D15

I can recognise and correct factual or structural errors in AI-generated content.

**Recommended action:** Treat AI output as a draft and validate important claims before reuse.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

### Safety

#### D16

I protect important accounts with appropriate authentication and recovery settings.

**Recommended action:** Review the authentication, recovery channels and active sessions of your primary email account.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D17

I keep devices and apps updated and know when a device is no longer supported.

**Recommended action:** Enable automatic updates where suitable and identify unsupported devices.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D18

I understand what personal or sensitive data I share with apps and services.

**Recommended action:** Review permissions and remove access that is not needed.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D19

I recognise when digital use harms concentration, wellbeing or healthy boundaries.

**Recommended action:** Set one concrete boundary for notifications, focus time or device use.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D20

Before giving data to an AI service, I consider confidentiality, privacy and necessity.

**Recommended action:** Avoid entering sensitive data unless service, purpose and permissions justify it.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

### Problem solving

#### D21

I can describe a digital problem clearly before trying random fixes.

**Recommended action:** Record the symptom, expected behaviour, recent change and exact error.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D22

I choose digital tools based on requirements rather than popularity alone.

**Recommended action:** Compare candidate tools against security, portability, cost and task requirements.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D23

I can learn a new digital tool using documentation, examples and controlled experimentation.

**Recommended action:** Learn one unfamiliar function from primary documentation.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D24

I know when a digital task exceeds my competence or authority and I should seek help.

**Recommended action:** Define stop conditions for security, legal, financial or irreversible actions.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`

#### D25

I can use AI to explore options while keeping responsibility for the final decision and verification.

**Recommended action:** Separate AI suggestions from evidence and the decision you personally accept.

**Sources:** `digcomp3`
